Frequently Asked Questions

What Macs does Unburn run on?
Unburn runs on any Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer) on macOS 12 Monterey or later. Intel Macs and Windows are not currently supported.
What kinds of sensor damage does it heal?
Unburn heals fixed dots and dot clusters that sit in the exact same place on every frame (laser burns, stuck hot pixels, burnt photosites). It does not heal continuous single-pixel line damage or moving dust bunnies.
Will it work with my camera?
Yes. A Sony ILCE-7M4 profile is built-in. For any other camera, run the Calibration Wizard once using 5–10 defocused shots of a wall/sky or 15–40 varied RAWs. Supported formats include Canon (.CR2, .CR3), Nikon (.NEF), Sony (.ARW), Fujifilm (.RAF), Olympus (.ORF), Panasonic (.RW2), Pentax (.PEF), Leica and Apple (.DNG), and Hasselblad (.3FR).
How does the Free Trial work?
The free download lets you test all calibration and healing features, inspect spots, and export test photos (with a subtle watermark). Entering a license key removes the watermark and unlocks full export.
First-time open instructions (Gatekeeper warning)
Because Unburn is an independent app distributed outside the App Store, macOS shows a warning on first launch. To open it: Right-click (Control-click) Unburn.app in Applications and choose Open, then click Open. On macOS 15, if prompted with Move to Bin, go to System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
